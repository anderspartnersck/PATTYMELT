# PATTY MELT

A grill-rush diner game. You are Patty. Billy is on expo and Billy is flaky.

## ▶ [PLAY IT IN YOUR BROWSER][play]

A Castle Killscreen game by Anders & Partners LLC.

## Modes

[1UP Diner][play] is the campaign and the place to start.

[Straight to the weeds][weeds] drops you into shift 9 for a feel test.

[2UP Diner][diner] is the two-player quarter-eater ·
[Pop-Up][popup] is the jailbreak cut ·
[Heroes][heroes] is the school kitchen ·
[Meltdome][battle] is the service battle.

## Controls

Arrow keys move between stations. F flips, C cheese, X toast bun, SPACE is
HEY BILLY. Full controls are printed along the bottom of the cabinet.
